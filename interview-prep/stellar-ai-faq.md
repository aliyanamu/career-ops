### Their bio rules (why the bio is shaped this way)

- ~200 words, professional summary. AI or annotation experience not required.
- Most recent role first, with employer AND dates.
- Name specific technologies, tools, methods used.
- Include education, degrees, certifications.
- Suggested skeleton: `[Role] at [employer] ([years]), where I [what you built] using [tools]. Before that, [earlier role and what you did]. [Degree] in [subject], [year].`
- Explicitly rejected: generic bios that only list task types ("interested in LLM evaluation, RLHF, creative writing and prompt engineering, detail oriented, open to qualification tests"). Says nothing about background.

## Qualification tests

- **Matching timeline:** passing the skill match test only puts you in the talent pool. No fixed timeline. Matching depends on client demand, project availability, and background fit. They email an onboarding invite when a project fits. Keeping the profile complete (current role + work experience) improves matching.
- **Test formats:** vary by project. Multiple choice, practical exercises, or sample tasks resembling the real work.
- **If you fail:** cooling-off period, then possibly eligible to retake. Watch the dashboard for it to reappear. Failing one does not affect eligibility for other projects.
- **Per project?** Not always. Qualifying for one type may auto-qualify you for similar projects. Specialized projects can require extra tests.
- **Timing:** start a qualification only when you have uninterrupted time. Qualifications are only accessible while both the qualification and the project are open; intake gets paused or projects put on hold per client needs.
- **Review lag:** results appear in the profile dashboard. Reviewed in order received, can take a while.
- **Payment:** one-time qualification bonus, issued after your submission is reviewed and approved. Framed as a paid take-home assessment.

## Guaranteed Hours contract

Only relevant later, offered to top contributors.

- **What:** contract (not employment) guaranteeing at least 20h/week of work at a predetermined minimum hourly rate for 6 months. Exact hours, rate, duration can vary.
- **Who:** no application. Offered to top contributors on select projects, typically after becoming a trusted reviewer on a project.
- **Rate:** they always pay the higher of the guaranteed rate and the project rate. Guaranteed $70/h on a $100/h project pays $100/h; on a $50/h project it still pays $70/h.
- **Short weeks:** allowed with advance notice by email to support. You are paid only for hours actually worked that week.
- **Termination:** either side, 5 days notice. They can end it if work stops meeting project requirements.
- **No fixed meetings or fixed availability.** You manage your own time, can work with other clients. Only requirement is the 20h getting done.
- **Extra hours:** allowed, but no guarantee more work is available.
- **If the project pauses:** you are still paid the minimum hours at the minimum rate each week.
