# DAT Self-Introduction (Project Admin / Software Engineer)

Hi, my name is Hana Aliyah Mufidah, you can call me Hana. I’m a full-stack engineer based in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, with about seven years of experience.

I originally studied Geophysical Engineering in college, but I graduated in 2015, right when oil and mining prices crashed, so it was a tough market for someone with my degree. For about two years I tried different things outside my field, but none of it matched where I actually felt strong, which was technical problem-solving, so I signed up for an immersive coding program in 2018.

Since then, I’ve worked across very different environments, from a small team building internal tools, to a large finance and trading company where I stayed on one product for over four years. There, I learned how to solve problems that actually mattered day to day, not just write code. Once, our database got corrupted, and our backup was two to three days stale with no way to recover what happened after, so I pulled every API request from the logs and replayed them in order to rebuild what was missing. That kind of care, making sure nothing gets lost even when the easy path would be to just restore the old backup and move on, became second nature to me. Most recently, I worked at a fintech company where I was directly responsible for core features, and I was given the opportunity to be result-driven by figuring out the path there myself, which pushed me to own problems fully rather than just execute on instructions.

That range, adjusting to very different environments while staying careful and thorough wherever I land, is what makes me confident I’d fit well as either a Project Admin or Software Engineer here.
