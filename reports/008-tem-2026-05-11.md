## Timezone Blocker — Key Open Question

The JD says: *"fully remote, distributed across Europe with clear core hours."*

- UK core hours (GMT+0/+1) vs GMT+7 = 6-7h offset → beyond Hana's 5h max
- CET core hours (GMT+1/+2) vs GMT+7 = 5-6h offset → borderline
- But: tem's own "Join our Mission" page says they "hire from different countries and cultures" (90+ team members globally)

**This is not a confirmed blocker** — it's an unverified one. "Distributed across Europe" may describe the current team composition, not a hiring restriction.

**Action before applying:** Ask the recruiter in the first call: "Is the engineering team hiring outside of European timezones? I'm based in Indonesia (GMT+7) — what does core hours overlap look like in practice?"

If confirmed workable → score rises to **4.0/5** (strong apply).
If Europe-only core hours required → 2.0/5 (hard blocker, no overlap possible).
