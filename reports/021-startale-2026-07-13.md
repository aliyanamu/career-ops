### Gaps

1. **"Large-scale production systems serving real users at meaningful scale / high traffic."** (Repeated 4-5x in the JD — clearly the load-bearing requirement.)
   - Hard blocker? No, but it is the real gap. Nespay is B2B payments and ION is internal work-management, neither is obviously consumer-scale high-traffic.
   - Adjacent experience: the indexer handles continuous on-chain data ingestion with outage recovery, dedup, and resource-conscious pruning. That is production reliability engineering, just not proven at "millions of consumer users" scale.
   - Mitigation: lead with the indexer's reliability story (forward + backward + cleanup, Redis dedup, configurable backfill after outages). Frame it as "designed for correctness under continuous load and outage recovery." Do not overclaim traffic numbers she does not have.

2. **Seniority framing.** JD is "Senior, take ownership with minimal oversight." No explicit YoE number is stated, so per _profile.md seniority policy this is acceptable (6.5 yrs, real end-to-end ownership at Nespay). The title is not a blocker; the scale expectation is the substance behind it.
   - Mitigation: position the Nespay indexer + backend as end-to-end ownership of critical infrastructure (she designed, built, and operates it). That is senior behavior regardless of title.

3. **PostgreSQL query optimization at scale.** She has Postgres and did schema/pruning design, but heavy query-optimization and migration tuning under load is not demonstrated.
   - Nice-to-have, not a blocker. Adjacent: index/cleanup design on the indexer. Speak to it honestly if asked.

**Net:** This is one of the tightest domain matches in the pipeline. TypeScript backend is her core stack, and "blockchain indexer + wallet + DeFi + Go" reads like her CV. The only genuine gap is proven consumer scale.

## C) Level & Strategy

**Detected level:** Senior IC, hands-on, high autonomy. Natural fit for the Web3 Backend archetype where she is strongest.

**Sell senior without stretching:**
- Lead with ownership, not title. "I designed, built, and operate the multi-chain indexer that relays on-chain payment data to our backend." Owning a critical service end-to-end is the senior signal.
- Emphasize the rare intersection: most backend engineers have not built a blockchain indexer, integrated wallets (Xellar, Privy), and worked on-chain data models. She has all three plus Go and TypeScript. That combination is exactly what this role asks for.
- Use the reliability framing: forward/backward indexers, outage recovery via configurable backfill, Redis dedup for exactly-once reporting, cleanup jobs to keep Postgres lean. This answers "fault tolerance, reliability, operational complexity" directly.

**If down-leveled (mid rather than senior):**
- Acceptable if comp is fair for remote-from-Indonesia (see Block D). Negotiate a 6-month review with clear promotion criteria tied to owning a consumer-facing service at scale.
- The scale gap is a legitimate reason a startup might pitch mid; that is fine given the domain upside and the learning surface (on-chain orderbook L1, wallet super-app).
