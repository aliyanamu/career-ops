## Q: What is it about tem's mission and/or ways of working made you want to apply? Please let us know what excites you about this opportunity!

What drew me to tem is pretty straightforward: energy bills are confusing on purpose, and a big chunk of what businesses pay goes to middlemen who didn't actually supply anything. tem is trying to fix that and give businesses a price that actually reflects what energy costs. That's a problem worth solving.

The transparency piece also connects to something I've been curious about from my payments work. In crypto you can see exactly what moved, where, and why. I wonder if energy could work the same way eventually, where you see what was supplied, what you used, and what you owe, all backed by real data. tem's focus on transparency made me curious whether that's a direction they're thinking about too.
